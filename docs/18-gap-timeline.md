# 18. Timeline of the work on the gap

**Status:** One table lists every try and idea of these notes against the gap to the paper, in the order of time. No new runs. The dates come from the documents. If a document gives no date, the date is the date of its first commit.

**The gap** ([document 7](07-results.md)): the Lizard model gets 23.3 on MMLU (paper: 29.8), 68.0 on PIQA (paper: 74.8) and 54.8 on ARC-Easy (paper: 65.6).

Documents 1–3 (the port and the infrastructure), the figures and the definitions have no row. They contain setup or reference material only.

## 3. What the timeline shows

- **Excluded causes.** The evidence excludes a bug in the attention code and missing checkpoint keys. It also excludes the checkpoint selection, the temperature and the feature dimension. The harness gives the teacher a normal MMLU score.
- **The drop starts in stage 1.** Stage 2 recovers approximately 10 PIQA points, with both stage 2 recipes.
- **A lower loss does not give a higher accuracy.** The stage 1 loss fell from 8.16 to 3.05 over the runs. PIQA and ARC-Easy did not improve.
- **The main open cause is the architecture:** a limit for each head, and a window without RoPE. The design options of rows 24–26 tested this cause only in part.
- **One inexpensive check is still open:** the teacher on PIQA and ARC-Easy in this harness (X0).
